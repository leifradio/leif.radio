RaDAR (Rapid Deployment Amateur Radio) was created by Eddie Leighton, ZS6BNE, in South Africa. It's an activity held twice a year, on the first Saturday of April and November, and it's all about setting up quickly, making five QSOs, then moving to a new location and doing the same thing again. This goes on for four hours, and the aim is to collect as many points as possible. Even though points are awarded, it isn't really a contest; it's more about challenging yourself and having fun.

You'll find the rules at [RaDAR Rally Rules](https://www.radarrally.info/p/radar-rally-rules.html) and general information at [radarrally.info](https://www.radarrally.info/).

The next event is on 2027-04-03, hope to hear you there!
