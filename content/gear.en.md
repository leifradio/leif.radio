## Portable

Used for portable operation.

- **Transceiver:** Lab599 TX-500MP with LiNK500MP mk3
- **Antenna:** Chameleon CHA LEFS Feather, lightweight end-fed half-wave (EFHW) wire antenna for portable QRP use.
