## Winlink Wednesday

Today is Wednesday, and that means Winlink Wednesday, a test that Winlink is working as it should. I operated from home on the usual antenna, but with the TX500MP and a computer connected wirelessly via the LiNK500MP mk3.

{{< figure src="tx500mp-link500.jpg" alt="TX500MP with LiNK500MP mk3 on 40 m" caption="TX500MP with LiNK500MP mk3 on 40 m" >}}

The activity involves sending an email over HF to a recipient who compiles a list of everyone who has checked in. This time it was the first Wednesday of the month, so in addition to the standard information such as name and QTH, I also reported the weather conditions.

The purpose is to test the ability to communicate by radio, something that's important to be able to do if other means of communication aren't available.

More info at [winlinkwednesday.net](https://winlinkwednesday.net/).
