Today is Winlink Wednesday and the third Wednesday of the month, which means a check-in with the ICS-213 General Message form. I connected via OH8STN, Julian, in Finland. I first tried a station in Germany, but a few people broke in with an SSB QSO, so I switched to Julian's station instead.

My station was as usual the TX-500MP with the LiNK500MP and the usual vertical at my home QTH. Power about 10 watts.

Information about the form: [Winlink Wednesday ICS-213 Check-In (PDF)](https://winlinkwednesday.net/docs/Winlink_Wednesday_ICS-213_Check-In.pdf)
