On Saturday I took the radio with me up to Gimsklack in Dalarna, Sweden. What a view!

I tried out the radio with a small wire antenna and heard lots of signals from around Europe. I hadn't brought enough warm clothes, and the cold meant I didn't try for any QSOs, but the [Reverse Beacon Network (RBN)](https://www.reversebeacon.net/) showed that I was getting out well on 20 meters.
